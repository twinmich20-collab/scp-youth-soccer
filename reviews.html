From a former coach

A letter from Union County College's Head Men's Soccer Coach, who has known SCPYS founder Rodrigo DaSilva for over 20 years.

Recognized statewide

In 2019, NJ Youth Soccer named Rodrigo DaSilva its Liesel Krehan Volunteer of the Year at the 32nd Annual NJYS Awards Dinner.

"We are a little club with big hearts, and our goal is to give everyone an opportunity to play ball," DaSilva said of the honor.

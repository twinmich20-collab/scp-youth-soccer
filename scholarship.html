In memoriam

Chico Memorial scholarship.

The SCP Youth Soccer Francisco "Chico" Silva Memorial Scholarship.

His legacy

The SCP Youth Soccer Francisco "Chico" Silva Memorial Scholarship celebrates and remembers the life of Francisco Silva — a passionate, dedicated player within the Luso soccer community of the Tri-State Area. Born in Portugal, Chico immigrated to the United States in the late 1970s and quickly became a fixture in the local soccer community, including Sport Club Portuguese, Barriadinos, and others. His love for the game extended beyond personal play — he was a mentor to younger players, a reliable teammate, and a knowledgeable student of the sport, competing in leagues such as L.I.S.A. and the Interstate Italian American Soccer League.

Chico's passion for soccer was matched only by his love for his family. He was a loving husband, adored father, and cherished grandfather, and his commitment to his family showed in every part of his life. To ensure his memory and passion for the game live on, this scholarship was created to support future generations of soccer players as they pursue their own dreams on the field.

At a glance

Scholarship details.

Award amount $500–$1,500
Application deadline June 1
Winners notified By July 1
Minimum essay 250 words

Awarded each year to promising male or female high school seniors, to assist with the costs of attending the college or trade school of their choice.

Eligibility

  • Current high school senior
  • Played a minimum of 2 full years on an SCPYS club team, or volunteered 1 full year with SCP Youth Soccer
  • Advanced coursework and programs are acknowledged and reviewed by the scholarship committee
  • Essay on how soccer has positively impacted your life, and how kindness, respect, and honesty — values important to Francisco — show up in your everyday life (see minimum length above)

Requirements

  • Official high school transcript showing a GPA of at least 3.5
  • A brief description of your planned area of study and career goal
  • Demonstrated commitment to volunteer service in our community
  • Acceptance to and enrollment in a two- or four-year college or trade school
Application process

How to apply.

1. Complete the form

Apply online, or use the fillable PDF — print, complete, and scan it if you prefer. Only applications filed online will be given consideration.

2. Attach everything

Transcript, essay, and letters of recommendation all go in with your application, submitted together as one PDF. Don't send materials separately.

3. Email it in

Send your complete application and all scanned attachments to scpyouthsoccer@gmail.com with the scholarship name in the subject line, by June 1.

Incomplete or late applications will not be considered. Applications are reviewed by the Scholarship Committee and fund holder based on commitment to service in our community, academic progress, and involvement in soccer. Keep a copy of your application for your records. Questions? Email scpyouthsoccer@gmail.com.

Travel program · U8–U15

Make the team.

A minimum of two sessions, evaluated on technical, physical, and coachability criteria. Here's exactly how SCPYS travel tryouts work.

Tryout sign-up opens Spring 2027
Step by step

How tryouts work.

All players must register online to be eligible. Parents are asked to stay off the tryout field so coaches can focus on players.

01
Register online

Players must register for the travel program before they're eligible to try out.

02
Attend 2 sessions

A minimum of two 60–90 minute sessions over a two-week window, for all ages U8–U15.

03
Get evaluated

Coaches score players through scrimmages and 4 skill stations: 1v1, passing & possession, finishing, and 4v4.

04
Roster decisions

Head coaches determine roster spots; all players are notified directly. Selected players commit to a full season — Fall, Winter, and Spring.

What coaches look for

Evaluation criteria.

Returning players are scored 50% on last season's performance, 50% on tryout day. New players are evaluated 100% on tryout performance.

01

Technical ability

Footwork, passing, and ball control — the fundamentals every station is built around.

02

Physical

Speed, quickness, agility, and strength across scrimmage and station play.

03

Coachability

Receptiveness to coaching and constructive feedback, and overall interest in the game.
